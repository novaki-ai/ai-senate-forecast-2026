## Alaska (AK) · Class 2 regular
Incumbent: Dan S. Sullivan (Republican), running.
Rules: Top-four nonpartisan primary (held Aug 18, 2026); the four finishers advance to a ranked-choice general election. Voters may rank all four candidates; if no candidate has a majority of first choices, the last-place candidate is eliminated and those ballots move to their next ranking, repeated until two remain. Ballots that rank no remaining candidate are exhausted. Mailed ballots postmarked by Election Day are counted if they arrive within the post-election receipt window, so the RCV tabulation is run after that window: in 2022 and 2024 it was run about two weeks after Election Day (Nov 23, 2022; Nov 20, 2024). Four names on the general ballot: Dan S. Sullivan (R), Mary Peltola (D), Daniel J. Sullivan Jr. (listed without party), Gerald L. Heikes (R). A U.S. Justice Department lawsuit filed Oct 2026 asks a court to block certification if Sen. Sullivan loses narrowly (see events).
